# ⚒️ Just Tools — Tool Expansion Done Right

**Just Tools** adds 110+ new items to Minecraft without changing the game's core feel. New tool types, two new material tiers, a powerful tool hardening system, and 11 unique upgrade plates that let you customize any tool.

---

## 🔨 New Tool Types

### Hammers — 3×3 Area Pickaxes
Mine a full 3×3 wall of stone in one swing! Available for every tier from Wood to Netherite. Only uses 1 durability per swing, regardless of how many blocks are broken.

### Excavators — 3×3 Area Shovels
Clear dirt, gravel, sand, and soul sand in massive 3×3 swathes. Perfect for terraforming and clearing land.

### Paxels — The Ultimate Multi-Tool
One tool that does it all: Pickaxe + Axe + Shovel + Hoe. Right-click to strip logs, flatten paths, scrape oxidation, remove wax, till soil, and extinguish campfires.

---

## ⛏️ New Materials: Copper & Deepslate

- **Copper Gear** — Fast mining speed (5.0), high enchantability (13), 190 tool durability. Complete tool set + 4-piece armor set!
- **Deepslate Gear** — Tanky early-game equipment with 320 tool durability and inherent Armor Toughness + Knockback Resistance.

Both tiers include the full set: Sword, Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel, Hoe, Hammer, Excavator, Paxel, Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, and Boots.

---

## 🦺 Armor Expansion & Hardened Armor

- **Copper & Deepslate Armor** — Finally, armor sets for early-to-mid game!
- **Hardened Armor (All 8 Tiers)** — Reinforce Leather, Copper, Deepslate, Chainmail, Iron, Gold, Diamond, or Netherite armor into Hardened variants with **2.5× to 3× durability** and bonus Toughness!
- **Armor Upgrade Plates** — Every single upgrade plate can be installed onto armor pieces (per-piece activation, stackable):
  - 🔥 **Lava Seal**: Fire & lava damage immunity
  - 💨 **Wind Core**: Complete fall damage immunity
  - 💎 **Amethyst Shield**: Prevents armor from ever shattering (stops at 1 durability)
  - 🧲 **Magnetic Vacuum**: 8-block item magnet aura
  - ⚡ **Redstone Turbine**: +15% movement speed boost
  - 🌋 **Thermal Core**: Flame barrier setting attackers ablaze + freeze immunity
  - 💜 **Soul Repair**: Passive auto-repair + XP mending
  - 🌿 **Photosynthesis**: Sunlight repair + daylight regeneration
  - 🔩 **Reinforced Frame**: 50% chance zero armor durability loss
  - 🛡️ **Heavy Plating (Depth)**: 12% incoming damage reduction per piece
- **Smithing Trims**: All 40 armor pieces fully support vanilla armor trims!

## 🛡️ Tool & Armor Hardening Station

A brand new workstation that lets you **reinforce any tool or armor piece** into a Hardened variant:

- **2.5× to 4× durability boost** — Diamond Chestplate goes from 528 to 1,360!
- **Better defense, speed, damage, and enchantability**
- **Free repairs** with raw materials (50% per item, 0 XP cost)
- **Gear combining** that merges enchantments + grants 12% bonus durability
- **Resets anvil penalty** on every operation — no more "Too Expensive!"

Works on all vanilla tools and armor AND all JustTools gear.
